# About

I've always been driven by challenges that push my boundaries. If it doesn't stretch my limits, it's not worth my time.

Inspired by the economic instability I grew up with in Argentina, I've always been passionate about fundamentally changing the global financial system, and I have not decided to look the other way. My academic path has been anything but linear. I started in engineering, dabbled in psychology, and along the way, picked up a diverse skill set that helps me orchestrate complex teams and solve challenging problems.

Learning and continuous improvement are at the heart of everything I do. I really enjoy working with teams that empower me and being hands-on. Outside of work, you'll probably find me preparing for my next marathon.

I'm currently working as Director of Operations at [Cambrian](https://www.cambrian.org/), the best source of real-time and historical onchain and offchain financial data built for agents, institutions and traders.
